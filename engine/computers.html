Who can open the page

Files stay on their own computer. The page and the catalogue are reached only over HTTPS with a certificate made on this computer, and every new phone or tablet must be approved here with a 6-digit code.

Hub

Make this computer the hub: other computers at home send their catalogues here, so one search covers everything. They need the hub to be reachable on home Wi-Fi (or your VPN).

Network folders instead

A computer or NAS that cannot run Universal Organiser (a NAS, an old PC) can still be included: share its folder on the network, connect it on this computer (Finder → Go → Connect to Server, File Explorer → Map network drive, or your Linux file manager), then add it in Settings → Folders & layout. Its files are then scanned from here.
