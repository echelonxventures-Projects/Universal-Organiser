1What to sort

The sorter files things where they belong using the catalogue's labels. It learns the destination from where your other files with the same owner and topic already are; otherwise it uses your layout preset. It never touches projects, apps or protected folders, never moves iCloud-only files out of iCloud, and never puts sensitive files into iCloud.
